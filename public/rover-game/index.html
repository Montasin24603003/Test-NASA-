1. BEKKER–WONG RIGID-WHEEL PRESSURE-SINKAGE EQUATION

z₀ = [ (3 · F_N) / ( b · (3 - n) · (k_c/b + k_φ) · √(2 · r) ) ] ^ ( 2 / (2n + 1) )

Calculates radial sinkage z₀ of a rigid cylindrical rover wheel under normal load F_N, wheel width b = 0.525 m, radius r = 0.2625 m, cohesive modulus k_c, frictional modulus k_φ, and sinkage exponent n. On Martian regolith (Loose Regolith), nominal static sinkage is ~1.35 cm; in active slip, digging factor (1 + 1.8 · s^1.4) increases sinkage up to 8.5+ cm in fine sand dunes.

2. MOHR–COULOMB & JANOSI–HANAMOTO SHEAR STRESS RELATION

τ(j) = ( c + σ · tan φ ) · ( 1 - exp(-j / K) )

Determines maximum soil traction shear envelope where c is soil cohesion, φ is internal friction angle, σ = F_N / A_contact is normal pressure, j = ∫ |v_slip| dt is shear displacement, and K is Janosi shear modulus (0.012–0.038 m). Rolling resistance compaction work is integrated via: R_c = (b · (k_c/b + k_φ) / (n + 1)) · z₀^(n + 1).

3. MARS BAROMETRIC HYDROSTATIC EQUATION & ATMOSPHERIC DENSITY

P(z) = P_datum · exp( -z_MOLA / H ) , where H = ( R_spec · T ) / g

ρ = P / ( R_spec · T ) [Ideal Gas Law for 95.3% CO₂ Atmosphere]

Anchored to mean Martian datum pressure P_datum = 610.0 Pa at MOLA 0 m areoid with R_spec = 191.18 J/(kg·K) and g = 3.71 m/s². At Jezero Crater floor (-2560 m), atmospheric pressure increases naturally to ~760 Pa with scale height H ≈ 12.8 km and density ρ ≈ 0.0156 kg/m³, matching NASA Mars 2020 MEDA pressure sensor observations.

4. BEER–LAMBERT ATMOSPHERIC SOLAR EXTINCTION

I_surface = S₀ · sin θ_sun · exp( -τ · m_air ) + I_diffuse

Mean top-of-atmosphere Martian solar irradiance S₀ = 590.0 W/m² at 1.524 AU is attenuated by atmospheric dust optical depth τ (derived from regional baseline + storm intensity) and optical airmass m_air = 1 / sin θ_sun. Diffuse sky radiation incorporates forward scattering through suspended atmospheric dust.

5. FOUR-NODE LUMPED THERMAL CAPACITANCE ODE NETWORK

C_i · ( dT_i / dt ) = Σ Q_internal - h_conv · A_i · ( T_i - T_mars ) - Q_rad

Governs temperature evolution across four nodes: (1) Battery Pack inside Warm Electronics Box (WEB) with internal resistance Joule heating I²·R and survival heaters, (2) Drive Actuator Motors exposed on wheel hubs cooled by forced convection h_conv ∝ √(ρ·v_wind), (3) Rover Compute Element (RCE) avionics, and (4) Mast / SuperCam Head exposed to solar flux and cold ambient air (-125°C to +20°C).

7. KNOWN SIMULATION APPROXIMATIONS

  • Terramechanics: Rigid-wheel analytical formulation rather than full discrete element method (DEM) granular continuum simulation, calibrated for real-time 120 Hz browser execution.
  • Electrical Pack: Time-scaled capacity multiplier applied so energy management and night survival gameplay remain engaging within 15–20 minute play sessions while retaining exact Volt-Ampere physics.
  • Atmosphere: Single-column 1D hydrostatic barometric lapse rate and harmonic diurnal MEDA fit rather than full 3D global circulation model (GCM).
